# Analyze a captured trace

Use for: Explain a profile, trace, heap snapshot, or crash artifact.

## Procedure

1. Identify the trace format, time range, capture method, build, and workload.
2. Use a compatible parser or viewer. Keep the original artifact unchanged.
3. Find dominant samples, resource owners, blocked paths, or lifecycle anomalies relevant to the question.
4. Map findings to the matching source revision where available.
5. Separate measured evidence from attribution that the trace cannot establish.
6. Report the finding with sample or event locations and the next capture needed to resolve gaps.
